## 1. Introduction

The **Kinetic Protocol** proposes a fundamental shift in how we handle decentralized namespaces. 
Instead of relying on fiat-pegged blockchain auctions to distribute domains, Kinetic uses *Proof of Patience*—a non-parallelizable cryptographic challenge known as a Verifiable Delay Function (VDF).

If you want to register one domain, it is computationally trivial for an average machine to wait for the VDF to resolve. However, if a malicious actor attempts a Sybil attack by registering millions of domains, they will run out of computational time, as VDFs cannot be sped up by adding more cores.

## 2. The Namespace Trilemma

Traditionally, Zooko's Triangle dictates that a naming system cannot simultaneously be:
- Human-meaningful
- Secure
- Decentralized

We solve this mathematically.

$$
T_{claim} = \mathcal{O}(D) + \text{VDF}(x, \Delta t)
$$

Where $D$ is the dictionary difficulty and $\Delta t$ is the dynamic time parameter anchored to the latest beacon state.

### Conclusion
By anchoring identities in *time* rather than *capital*, Kinetic ensures that the internet remains equally accessible to anyone with a standard CPU.
